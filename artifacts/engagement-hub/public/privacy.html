Privacy Policy

C9MYR Employee's Hub — last updated September 2026

C9MYR Employee's Hub ("the app") is an internal tool used by C9MYR employees and authorized contacts to track goals, challenges, and team engagement. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Your email address and basic profile info (name, profile photo) from whichever sign-in method you use — email link, password, or a third-party sign-in like Google.
  • Content you post in the app: goals, challenges, social posts, comments, and reactions.
  • Usage data needed for the app's features to work, such as points, streaks, and badges.

How it's used

Information is used only to operate the app's features for its intended purpose — showing your goals and posts to your coworkers, tracking engagement points, and letting admins manage accounts and content. We do not sell or share your data with outside parties, and we don't use it for advertising.

Third-party sign-in

If you sign in with Google (or another third-party provider we add in the future), we only receive the basic profile information needed to create your account (name, email, profile photo) — never your password with that provider.

Data retention & access

Your data is stored for as long as your account is active. Workspace admins can view and moderate account and content data as part of running the app. You can contact an admin to request account deletion.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out to your workspace admin.
